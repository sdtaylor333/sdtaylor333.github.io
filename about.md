About S. D. Taylor

Interdisciplinary Scholar | Researcher | Technology Enthusiast

Welcome to my personal website, a growing digital library dedicated to intellectual exploration, independent scholarship, and the exchange of ideas.

My academic background includes a Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Studies from Liberty University and a Master of Arts in Theological Studies from Liberty Theological Seminary.

My interests extend across the humanities, behavioral sciences, biological sciences, and technology. I am particularly interested in the connections between disciplines and how different fields of knowledge contribute to our understanding of human thought, behavior, consciousness, and experience.

Academic and Research Interests

Philosophy and Theology

Biblical theology, soteriology, union with Christ, philosophy of religion, epistemology, metaphysics, and the history of philosophical thought.

Psychology and History of Psychology

Historical schools of psychology, behaviorism, cognitive psychology, learning theory, hypnosis, consciousness, and the philosophical foundations of psychological science.

Psychopharmacology and Neuroscience

Neurotransmitter systems, receptor pharmacology, pharmacodynamics, pharmacokinetics, neurobiology, and the scientific study of psychoactive substances.

Biochemistry and Amino Acids

Amino acid metabolism, biochemical pathways, neurotransmitter synthesis, and the relationship between molecular biology and nervous system function.

Biblical Koine Greek

New Testament vocabulary, lexical analysis, biblical interpretation, and the historical and theological significance of Greek terminology.

Technology and Artificial Intelligence

Computer technology, digital research tools, website development, artificial intelligence, and emerging applications of AI in education, scholarship, and scientific research.

Independent Scholarship

My research interests include the nature of saving faith, union with Christ, the kingdom of God, and salvation understood as participation in the life of the risen Christ.

I am also developing an interdisciplinary educational reference library, beginning with an alphabetical encyclopedia of the history of psychology.

This project will expand to include philosophical studies, biblical Greek word studies, psychopharmacology, neuroscience, and biochemistry.

Technology and Digital Projects

I have a longstanding interest in technology and its practical applications. This website serves as a platform for developing digital reference materials, organizing research, exploring web technologies, and experimenting with emerging AI-assisted educational tools.

Future additions may include technology projects, professional experience, a technical résumé, and commentary on artificial intelligence.

The Purpose of This Website

This website is a personal intellectual workspace and a developing public resource.

Its purpose is simple: to explore ideas, connect disciplines, preserve research, and make knowledge accessible.

Learning is a lifelong undertaking, and intellectual curiosity does not have an expiration date.
