# Wilhelm Wundt (1832–1920)

**German physiologist, philosopher, and pioneer of experimental psychology.**

Wilhelm Maximilian Wundt is widely recognized as a foundational figure in experimental psychology.

In 1879, Wundt established a laboratory at the University of Leipzig devoted to experimental psychological research. His work helped establish psychology as an independent scientific discipline.

## Major Contributions

- Development of experimental approaches to psychological research.
- Investigation of sensation, perception, attention, and conscious experience.
- Establishment of an influential laboratory for training psychological researchers.
- Development of a broader cultural psychology (*Völkerpsychologie*).

## Historical Significance

Wundt helped transform psychology into an experimental discipline while recognizing that complex cultural and psychological processes required additional methods of investigation.
